# Styles and flags

Every flag has a long form; the short forms cover the frequent ones.

## Wrapping flags

`--width N` rewraps at N columns; `--no-wrap` passes long lines through.
The details live on the [wrapping page](wrapping.md).

## Colour flags

`--rules FILE` loads named patterns with a colour each, in the format
described under [colour rules](colour-rules.md). Rules combine with the
presets from the [preset list](presets.md).
